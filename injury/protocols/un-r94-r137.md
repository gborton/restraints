# UN Regulations No. 94 (Offset Frontal) and No. 137 (Full Width Frontal)

Type-approval pass/fail requirements under the 1958 Agreement. Only the original UNECE texts are
legally authentic. The texts used here are EU Official Journal consolidations [S9, S23]. Check
the UNECE status document (ECE/TRANS/WP.29/343) for the current series.

**Filters** [S23 Annex 3 §5.2]: head 1000; neck tension/shear 1000; neck moment 600; chest
deflection 180; femur/tibia forces and moments 600; knee slider 180.

**VC** [S23 Annex 4 §6]: deflection filtered once at CFC 180, D in metres, δt ≤ 1.25 × 10⁻⁴ s,
`(V*C)(t) = 1.3 × V(t) × C(t)`, C = D/0.229.

**TI** [S23 Annex 4 §5]: (M_C)_R 225 Nm, (F_C)_Z 35.9 kN. Fz may come from either tibia location
and is used for both TI calculations.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|

**Filters** [S9 Annex 3 §5.2]: head 1000; neck tension/shear 1000; neck moment 600; chest
deflection 180; femur 600. VC [S9 Annex 4 §5]: D filtered once at CFC 180, D_const 0.229 (50M) /
0.187 (5F), same procedure as R94.

## Neck moment location

R94/R137 define the bending moment "about a lateral axis at the neck/head interface" [S9, S23
Annex 3 §5.2.2.2]. In practice this is the occipital-condyle moment M_OCy = My − 0.01778·Fx (as in
Euro NCAP CP 005 [S6] and the NHTSA NCAP lab procedure [S24]). ⚠ The regulation text does not
state the transfer equation.
